Transparent metering

What counts as a
qualified lead?

Not every contact in a LinkedIn search is worth your time. GTM-OS runs every prospect through a strict 7-gate pipeline before it counts toward your quota.

The 7-Gate Pipeline

1
Dedup
Already in your database? Skipped immediately.
2
Headline
Title and role must match your ICP target seniority.
3
Exclusion
Blocklist, competitors, and spam signals filtered out.
4
Company
Company size, industry, funding stage checked against ICP.
5
Enrichment
Missing contact data pulled via provider registry.
6
AI Score (Claude)
Scored 0–100 with your ICP rules + intelligence context. Must reach ≥ 65.
7
Threshold
Final pass/fail. Only score ≥ 65 enters your campaign.
🎯

You never pay for junk contacts

A lead counts toward your quota only after passing all 7 gates and scoring ≥ 65. Contacts that bounce on Gate 1 (dedup) or Gate 3 (exclusion) cost you nothing.

🏗️

Trade fair leads get a boost

Badge-scanned visitors rated hot by your staff get +15 pts in Gate 6. Demo attendees get +10. These warm leads qualify faster — and your staff rating is preserved in the intelligence store.

📈

Overage is capped and predictable

Once you exceed your plan quota, each additional qualified lead costs €0.35. No tier jumps, no surprise invoices. You see the meter in real time from the campaign dashboard.

Frequently asked questions

Anything not answered here — just email us.

How exactly is a "qualified lead" counted?

A lead is counted when it passes all 7 gates and receives an AI score of 65 or above (Gate 7). Contacts that are filtered out at any earlier gate — duplicate, wrong title, competitor, wrong company size, unenrichable, or low AI score — are never counted. The meter resets on your billing anniversary each month.

What happens when I hit my quota mid-month?

Campaigns continue running without interruption. Each additional qualified lead is billed at €0.35 and shown on your next invoice. You can set a soft cap in the campaign settings to pause intake automatically if you prefer a hard budget.

Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and you're prorated for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. Annual plan upgrades are also prorated.

Do I need a LinkedIn premium or Sales Navigator account?

LinkedIn automation runs through Unipile — you provide your LinkedIn credentials and GTM-OS manages connect request pacing (max 30/day, within LinkedIn's safe limits). Sales Navigator is optional but improves search depth. You do not need a LinkedIn API key.

How does the trade fair module work?

Available on Scale and Enterprise. After your exhibition, export your badge scanner CSV and run import-badge-scans. GTM-OS normalises 30+ column formats, deduplicates by email, and feeds each visitor into the 7-gate pipeline. Staff ratings (hot/warm/cold) and demo attendance feed into Gate 6 scoring to boost warm contacts. The fair-followup sequence fires day 0 (connect + personal note), day 1 (first DM), day 4 (follow-up), and day 10 (email). Intelligence is tagged to the fair and set to expire after 18 months.

Is my data stored on your servers?

GTM-OS is open-source and self-hosted. Your leads database, intelligence store, and campaign data live in a SQLite file on your own machine or server. We never hold your contact data. The only external calls are to the AI providers (Anthropic) and enrichment services you configure.

What is the "dream cycle"?

The dream cycle is GTM-OS's overnight learning loop. While you sleep, Claude reviews all recent replies and engagement signals, promotes hypotheses to validated insights, contradicts stale beliefs, archives expired intelligence, and rebuilds the vector index. The next day's outreach scores from a smarter baseline. It runs on a launchd schedule (macOS) or cron job — you can also trigger it manually.
