Stage 2 — 7-Gate Qualification Pipeline
leads:qualify
G0
Deduplication –5%
Cross-checks existing leads & campaigns. Flags ambiguous for Notion review.
G1
Headline Pre-qual –30%
Filters by job title / role keywords from ICP rules file.
G2
Exclusion List –5%
Removes blacklisted names, companies, domains, URLs.
G3
Company Disqualifiers –5%
Blocks excluded companies, wrong industries, competitors.
G4
Enrichment FullEnrich · Unipile
Adds missing email, LinkedIn, company data. LinkedIn sources via Unipile.
G5
AI Qualification Claude Opus
Scores lead against ICP 0–100. Injects top validated intelligence insights.
G6
Score Threshold –40%
Keeps leads with score ≥ 50/100. Configurable per tenant.

Stage 3 — Message Generation & Validation
dev:outreach
Claude generates the message
1 unique message per prospect
Reads lead's signal, score, and ICP profile
Injects top 5 proven + 3 validated intelligence insights
Matches tone of voice from voice/tone-of-voice.md
Generates connect note (LinkedIn) + email variant
"Congratulations on your new role — we have a 3-bed apartment ready Q1 2027 in your target neighbourhood that matches your profile exactly. Worth a private viewing before it goes to public listing?"
8-rule validation
Hard rules — no exceptions
No word "signal" in copy
Never start with "I"
No disclaimers or filler phrases
No dash punctuation
No "nice to connect" filler
Start with "Hello"
No company TLDs (anti-spam)
Specific actionable CTA required
Fails validation → regenerated, not sent
Stage 4 — Outreach Sequence
30 LinkedIn connects / day
LinkedIn via Unipile
D+0 Connect request connect_note
Personalised note anchored on the lead's signal. Max 300 chars.
D+2 DM1 — Value open linkedin_dm
First direct message after connection accepted. Leads with the value, ends with soft CTA.
D+5 DM2 — Follow-up linkedin_dm
Final touch if no reply. Different angle. Auto-stops if reply detected at any step.
Email via Instantly / Brevo / SendGrid
D+0 Email 1 — Brief email
Personalised property / opportunity brief. Includes assets and CTA link.
D+3 Email 2 — Social proof email
Different angle, adds social proof or urgency signal. Skipped if email bounces.
Bounce detected → email steps skipped, LinkedIn continues
30 LinkedIn connects / day (rate limiter token bucket)
Sequence stops immediately on any reply
Never DM if prospect already replied
