Track 1 — Tenant Acquisition (Lease-Up)

Identify expanding companies
before they sign with a competitor
for your commercial spaces.

GTM-OS continuously monitors expansion and relocation signals — hiring surges, fundraising rounds, geographic growth — then triggers personalised lease-up outreach targeting COOs, CFOs, and Heads of Real Estate.

Hiring surge → office footprint expansion
+30% headcount growth over 6 months signals imminent need for additional floor space. Immediate outreach to COO or Head of Facilities.
Series A/B fundraising → new HQ budget
A funding round > €5M almost always precedes a HQ move or expansion. GTM-OS targets the CFO within 72h of the announcement.
Geographic expansion into your market
Companies opening their first local office or expanding into your city. Your development is their natural landing point.
Lease expiry approaching (public filings)
Crustdata cross-references job postings mentioning "office relocation" or "new HQ". 6–12 months window — optimal engagement timing.
