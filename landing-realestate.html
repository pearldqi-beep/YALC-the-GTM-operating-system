Track 1 — Buyer Sourcing & Outreach

Identify buyers at key life moments
before they visit a competitor's showroom
for your development.

GTM-OS continuously monitors personal relocation and life-event signals — job moves, family expansion, rental lease endings — then triggers personalised outreach to reach the right buyer at exactly the right moment.

Job relocation → new city, needs to buy
Firecrawl detects LinkedIn announcements of new roles in your city. A job move = a home purchase within 3 months. Outreach before they visit any showroom.
Family expansion → needs larger home
Marriage, birth, or inheritance signals from LinkedIn and press. Life events are the #1 driver of property purchase decisions — GTM-OS catches them in real time.
Rental lease ending → prime conversion window
Crustdata cross-references rental activity and lease cycle data. 6 months before lease renewal is the optimal moment to convert a renter into a buyer.
Active property search → high purchase intent
Profiles actively engaging with property content or recently promoted to a senior role signal purchase readiness. GTM-OS scores and prioritises them for immediate outreach.
