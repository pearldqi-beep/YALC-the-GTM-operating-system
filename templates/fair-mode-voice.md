# Fair Mode — Tone of Voice Preset

## When to use
Apply this preset for any outreach to leads sourced at a trade fair, exhibition,
or conference. Do NOT use cold-outreach framing — there is an existing shared
real-world context.

## Core principles

1. **Reference the shared event.** Every first message must name the fair and,
   where available, what was shown or discussed at the booth.

2. **Warm, not cold.** The lead chose to visit your booth. Treat them as a warm
   contact, not a prospect to be convinced. Tone is conversational, not
   pitch-heavy.

3. **Short and specific.** Connection notes: ≤ 280 characters. DMs: ≤ 4 short
   paragraphs. No walls of text.

4. **Avoid generic openers.** Never use:
   - "I noticed your company..."
   - "I came across your profile..."
   - "I hope this message finds you well..."
   These signal that you don't remember meeting them. Use the event as the anchor.

5. **One clear ask per message.** A call, a reply, or a "happy to send more
   details" — pick one. Do not stack multiple requests.

## Message tone markers

| Context | Language style |
|---|---|
| Connect note | Warm recognition — "great meeting you at [Fair]" |
| DM1 | Friendly follow-up — reference what was discussed |
| DM2 | Light persistence — acknowledge they're busy, offer something concrete |
| Email | Professional, direct — one value prop, one ask |

## What NOT to say
- Do not mention cold outreach metrics ("reply rate", "pipeline")
- Do not use urgency pressure ("limited time", "before it's too late")
- Do not over-pitch — they've already seen the product

## Booth interaction personalisation (when data is available)
- `demo_attended = true`: Reference the demo by name — "the live demo we showed you"
- `staff_rating = hot`: Lead showed high interest — push for a call sooner (DM1)
- `staff_rating = cold`: Keep DM1 lighter — share a resource rather than asking for a meeting
- `materials_collected`: Reference specific collateral they took — "the brochure on X"
- `session_attended`: Reference sessions they joined — "after the session on Y"
