How it works

Four steps. Fully automated.

We configure the pipeline for your sector and ICP, then run it continuously on your behalf.

01

Detect intent signals

We monitor funding rounds, product launches, job postings, contract awards, and 20+ other signals specific to your sector — in real time.

02

Qualify at scale

Every prospect passes a 7-gate AI pipeline — dedup, role filter, company fit, enrichment, and a 0–100 score aligned to your ICP. Only top fits reach outreach.

03

Reach with personalised sequences

AI-drafted messages anchored to the detected signal — LinkedIn DM + email follow-up — sent at the right cadence. Every message feels hand-written.

04

Track and optimise

Slack alert the moment a prospect replies positively. A/B testing, reply-rate tracking, and weekly reports let us continuously improve performance.

gtm-os pipeline

# 01 — Signal detection

❯ gtm signals:scan --sector energy

✓ 14 funding events · 8 contract awards · 3 product launches

# 02 — 7-gate qualification

❯ gtm qualify --limit 500 --min-score 72

✓ 500 in → 87 qualified (avg score: 81/100)

# 03 — Personalised outreach

❯ gtm campaign:launch --channel linkedin,email

✓ 87 sequences active · 30 connects/day rate-limited

# 04 — Live tracking

❯ gtm track --notify slack
