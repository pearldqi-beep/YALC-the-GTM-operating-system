How it works

Four steps. Fully automated.

We configure the pipeline for your sector and ICP, then run it continuously on your behalf.

1

Detect intent signals

We monitor funding rounds, product launches, job postings, contract awards, and 20+ other signals specific to your sector — in real time.

2

Qualify at scale

Every prospect passes a 7-gate AI pipeline — dedup, role filter, company fit, enrichment, and a Claude 0–100 score aligned to your ICP. Only the top fits reach outreach.

3

Reach with personalised sequences

AI-drafted messages anchored to the detected signal — LinkedIn DM + email follow-up — sent at the right cadence, automatically. Every message feels hand-written.

4

Track and optimise

You get a Slack alert the moment a prospect replies positively. A/B testing, reply-rate tracking, and weekly reports let us continuously improve your pipeline performance.

gtm-os pipeline

# Step 1 — Signal detection

gtm signals:scan --sector energy

✓ 14 funding events · 8 contract awards · 3 product launches


# Step 2 — 7-gate qualification

gtm qualify --limit 500 --min-score 72

✓ 500 in → 87 qualified (gate 6 avg score: 81/100)


# Step 3 — Personalised outreach

gtm campaign:launch --channel linkedin,email

✓ 87 sequences active · 30 connects/day rate-limited


# Step 4 — Live tracking

gtm track --notify slack

🔔 New reply: J. Martin · Head of Procurement

→ "Interested, let's talk next week."
